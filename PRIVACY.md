# HarmonyDocs privacy notice

## Current public-tool model

HarmonyDocs is a free public document-tool service. It does not require account registration for normal tool use.

This notice describes the product behaviour that must be implemented and verified in the deployed application. It does not make claims about encryption, automatic deletion, malware scanning, or other controls unless the active deployment has been configured and tested to provide them.

## Documents and processing

- HarmonyDocs must show visitors whether a tool runs in the browser or sends a file to a server before upload or processing begins.
- Document contents, filenames, extracted text, OCR output, passwords, and document metadata must not be used for advertising, profiling, marketing, lead scoring, or analytics.
- Public tools must not require an email address or marketing consent to process or download a document.
- If a tool stores a file or output on a server, the deployed service must state the retention period and provide a deletion process. Until that feature is live and tested, the product must not promise automatic deletion.

## Voluntary consultation requests

A visitor may choose to submit a consultation request. We collect only the fields displayed on the form: name, email address, optional phone number, organisation, country, service interest, project description, preferred contact method, consent choices, submission time, source CTA/tool, and campaign parameters where supplied in the URL.

Marketing permission is optional and must be separate from the privacy consent required to submit a consultation request. It must never be preselected.

## What is not collected for leads or analytics

Lead records and public analytics must not contain uploaded files, filenames, document contents, OCR text, passwords, full IP addresses, authentication tokens, or persistent cross-site identifiers.

## Contact

Questions about this notice or a consultation request should be directed to Harmony Digital Consults through the published contact channels in HarmonyDocs.
